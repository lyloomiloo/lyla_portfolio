Background

Scoot is the low-cost carrier arm of Singapore Airlines. It operates globally with 13 key markets and 9 different languages. They run a mix of performance and branding campaigns that require both scale and personality.

Marketing challenges, Magnific opportunities

  1. Mass illustration generation and contextualisation
  2. Strategic messaging variations per marketing funnel stage
  3. Transcreation across markets with different languages, air fares, destination matches, etc.
  4. Stock image integration (Scoot was a Freepik client!)

Solution in a nutshell

  1. Illustration codified. New replicable style with config, prompt and references locked.
  2. Workflows & spaces. Performance campaign with 4 distinct funnels set up as a use-case example for scaled work.

Results of both are encapsulated in the Playbook and the Wave Board.
