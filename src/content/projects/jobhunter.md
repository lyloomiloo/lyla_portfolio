## The Problem

The job market is brutal. AI is reshaping roles, companies hire and lay off in the same quarter, and the gap between "qualified" and "callback" has never been wider. The tools designed to help — job boards, LinkedIn, recruiter inboxes — optimise for volume, not for the person searching.

## What It Does

JobHunter scrapes hundreds of sources every six hours — Arbeitnow, Remotive, Adzuna across 17 countries, 125+ RSS feeds including direct career pages. Every listing is scored against your profile on six dimensions: role fit, skills, location, salary, company size, and personal values. Hard filters remove anything that doesn't clear your basics before a job reaches your feed.

## Pip

Pip is a soft, reactive blob on the home screen. Every session, Pip checks in: how are you feeling? Anxious? The feed opens with a breathing exercise. Motivated? Pip nudges you toward top matches. Drained? Pip tells you it's okay to close the tab.

Pip is also a literal stress ball. Tap it as many times as you want — it squishes, bounces back, makes a face.

## Adjacent Roles

In a market where roles change faster than CVs, JobHunter surfaces adjacent positions — roles with significant skill overlap you could step into. Brand strategist? It might flag UX researcher roles. The goal: expand your search without diluting it.

## Design Philosophy

Streaks and XP reward effort, not outcomes. You earn points for showing up, saving a job, or applying — not for callbacks you can't control. Confetti when you apply, because that's worth celebrating regardless of what happens next.

Free to use. Self-hosted. Your data stays yours.
