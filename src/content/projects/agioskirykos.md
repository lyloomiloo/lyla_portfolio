A tiny browser game set in Agios Kirykos, the port town of Ikaria. You play as Niko, who steps off the ferry and spends five short chapters doing what you actually do there: coffee on the square, errands for the baker and the beekeeper, petting every cat, cycling the harbour road, collecting shells, swimming off the beach south of the marina, and dancing at a panigiri until the ferry home. The town is laid out from the real map, and the details come from my own photos and memories — the cicadas, the stairs between lanes, neighbours calling out "ela!" and "ti kaneis?" as you pass. Built as a single HTML file with three.js, low-poly models from Polyfork, and voices made in ElevenLabs. Runs in the browser, nothing to install.
