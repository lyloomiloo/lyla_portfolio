## The Evolution

(re)Route 1.0 asked: what if navigation optimised for quality, not speed? Version 2.0 goes further — what if navigation wasn't about getting somewhere at all?

## How It Works

Open the app. A deck of quest cards greets you — LIVE events happening now, a daily community challenge, a personalised quest built from your taste profile, and AI-generated local discoveries. Pick one. Walk. Snap photos. The app learns what you love.

### Quest Types

**VISIT** — Walk to real places. 3-5 locations with proximity verification at 80m. A compass ring points to the next stop.

**WANDER** — Interpretive prompts. Walk and snap photos matching a vibe. AI judges each snap with vision.

**COLLECT** — Concrete scavenger hunts. Photograph specific things (doors, tiles, dogs). Gemini vision classifies each photo.

**LIVE** — Time-sensitive, ephemeral. Real events, recurring rhythms, golden hour windows. Generated fresh, never repeated.

## Taste Memory

The app remembers. Every query, every completed quest, every photo caption feeds a taste profile — rebuilt nightly by AI into a 150-word portrait of how you explore. Tomorrow's quests reflect today's discoveries.

## The Data

372 hand-curated POIs across Barcelona. Architecture, hidden courtyards, public art, viewpoints, gardens. Plus Google Places for the rest. The system knows when to use its own taste and when to search the world.

## What's New from v1

Native app (React Native + Expo). Quest system with 4 types. Photo snaps with AI validation. Taste memory that evolves. Badge system (24 achievements + 15 collections). Community daily quests. LIVE events via web search. Push notification infrastructure.
